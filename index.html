About us

IEEE

IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is the world's largest technical professional organization, with the tagline "Advancing Technology for Humanity". Its members are engineers, computer scientists, researchers and students all over the world.

IEEE Computer Society

The IEEE Computer Society (IEEE CS) is IEEE's community for computing: software, AI, cybersecurity, data, hardware and more. It connects students and professionals through conferences, publications and local chapters.
