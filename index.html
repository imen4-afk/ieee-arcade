About us

IEEE

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Its members are engineers, scientists, researchers and students all over the world.

IEEE Computer Society

The IEEE Computer Society (IEEE CS) is IEEE's community for computing: software, AI, cybersecurity, data, hardware and more. It connects students and professionals through conferences, publications and local chapters.
